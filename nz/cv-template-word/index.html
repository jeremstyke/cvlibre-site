How to fill in this CV template

Replace every text in square brackets with your own details, then delete the brackets. The headings and bullet points use real Word styles: to add a job, copy an existing block and paste it underneath, and the formatting follows.

  • Two pages at most: Careers New Zealand recommends keeping your CV to two pages. These templates fit on one page with room to grow.
  • No photo: Careers New Zealand advises against photos and images. Also leave out your date of birth or age, your marital status, your religion and your bank account details.
  • Personal statement: optional, but useful. Three or four lines on who you are, what you offer and the role you want.
  • Skills and experience: list your skills, then your jobs and volunteer work, most recent first. Back up each skill with an example.
  • Qualifications: give your NCEA level or your tertiary qualification, plus short courses that matter for the job.
  • Referees: you need at least two, and one should be your current manager or team leader if you have one. You can also write "Referees available on request".
  • Style: one black, easy-to-read font, no slang or abbreviations, and British spelling such as organised and licence.

Which template should I pick?

The classic CV works for almost every job. The modern CV suits people who want their target role and strongest skills at the top, for example when moving into a new industry. The first job CV is for school leavers and students: sport, kapa haka, a part-time job or volunteering all show skills an employer wants.

The elegant and two-column designs stand out when you email your CV or hand it in. For online application portals, the classic template is the safest choice, because some systems read columns in the wrong order.

Before you send your CV

Check the spelling carefully, then save your CV as a PDF (File, Save As, PDF) unless the employer asks for Word. Give it a clear name such as "Firstname-Lastname-CV.pdf", and tailor your personal statement and skills to each job ad.

Tips for editing the template in Word

To add a bullet point, press Enter at the end of an existing one. To move a whole section, select its heading and the lines below it, then cut and paste. If your text spills onto a second page, shorten older jobs to one or two bullet points before you make the font smaller: 10 points is about as small as you can go and stay easy to read. The page is set to A4, so it prints correctly on standard paper, and the margins are about 1.8 cm.

Frequently asked questions

Are these Word CV templates really free?

Yes. No account, no email and no payment: download the .docx file and use it for your applications.

Should I put a photo on my CV in New Zealand?

No. Careers New Zealand advises against photos, and employers do not expect one. Leave out your date of birth and marital status too.

How many referees do I need on a NZ CV?

At least two. Ideally one is your current manager or team leader. Ask them first, and check their phone numbers are up to date.
