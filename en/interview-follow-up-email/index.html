When to send a follow-up email

Send a short thank-you email within 24 hours of your interview. If you have heard nothing after the date they gave you, a polite follow-up about a week later is fine.

What to write

  • Thank them for their time, with the date of the interview.
  • Mention one thing you discussed that confirms your interest.
  • Ask politely about the next steps.

Keep it to a few lines. The goal is to leave a good impression, not to repeat your whole application.
