Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

Yes, 100%. All templates, PDFs without a watermark and the AI tools are free, with no card needed. A free account (an email address, no password) is optional: it keeps your CVs and cover letters online. CVLibre is funded by advertising and voluntary donations.

Is my information sent anywhere?

No, unless you choose to use the AI. Your CV is built on your device. Only the text or file you choose to send to the AI is sent off to be processed, and we do not keep it.

Should I put a photo on my CV?

In the UK, Ireland and the US, no: most employers prefer CVs without a photo, to avoid any bias. The photo is optional in CVLibre and the templates look good without one.

How long should a CV be?

Two A4 pages is the usual maximum in the UK. One page is fine for students, school leavers and people with little experience. CVLibre warns you if your CV goes over two pages.

What is an ATS-friendly CV?

An ATS (applicant tracking system) is software that sorts applications. An ATS-friendly CV contains real text, clear section headings and no images in place of text. All CVLibre templates work this way.

How do I save the PDF?

Click "Download PDF", choose "Save as PDF" instead of a printer, and untick "Headers and footers" for a clean result.
