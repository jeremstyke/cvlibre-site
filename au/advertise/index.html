Advertising and partnerships

CVLibre is a free resume and cover letter builder used in Australia, the United States, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Germany and France. Our visitors are looking for a job, an apprenticeship or a course, and they are with us at the moment they take action.

What we offer

  • Sponsored article: a genuinely useful guide on our blog, written with you and clearly labelled as a partnership.
  • Featured placement: your offer or service shown on the pages that match it (jobs, cover letters, resume advice).
  • Partnership: schools, training providers, recruitment agencies, job boards, tools for job seekers. We build an offer together that really helps our visitors.
  • Affiliate: a tracked link to your service, paid on performance.

Our rules

Sponsored content is always labelled as such. We turn down misleading offers, jobs or courses that charge candidates to apply, and anything unlawful. We never sell or share our users' resumes or data.
