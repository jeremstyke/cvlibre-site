A free service

CVLibre lets you create a resume and a cover letter and download them as PDFs, for free, with no card. Everything is open to everyone: the 33 resume templates, customisation (fonts, size, spacing), PDFs with no banner or watermark, and the AI tools.

CVLibre is funded by advertising and by the optional CVLibre Pass.

The CVLibre Pass

The Pass is a one-time payment for 3 months, with no subscription and no automatic renewal. It gives you 50 AI uses a day instead of 5, resume tailoring to a job ad, an ATS score, resume translation, up to 20 saved resumes instead of one, and browsing with no ads. It is linked to the CVLibre account used to buy it and is activated as soon as payment is received.

Payment is handled by Gumroad, which sells the Pass as merchant of record: Gumroad takes the payment, applies any sales tax or VAT and provides the receipt and invoice. For any question about the Pass or a refund request: contact@cvlibre.com.

The free account

An account is optional. It keeps up to 20 resumes and 30 cover letters online so you can find them on all your devices. You can delete each document, or your whole account, at any time.

Artificial intelligence

The AI helps you fill in a resume, improve a text, tailor a resume to a job ad and write a cover letter. Each use has a real cost for us: to keep the service free, it is limited to 5 uses per day per person, and it may be temporarily unavailable when demand is high. The AI can make mistakes: always read and correct the result. You remain responsible for what you send.

Fair use

Do not use CVLibre to create misleading documents or documents in someone else's name without their consent, or to send the AI documents that are not a resume (for example, identity documents). We may limit access in case of abuse.
