How to fill in this resume template

Replace every text in square brackets with your own details, then delete the brackets. The headings and bullet points use real Word styles: to add a job, copy an existing block and paste it underneath, and the formatting follows.

  • Two pages at most: the Department of Employment and Workplace Relations suggests keeping your resume to no more than two pages. If it runs to a second page, put your name, phone and email in the page header so they appear on every page.
  • Font: a simple font such as Calibri or Arial at 11 points. The templates already use Calibri.
  • No photo or personal details: Australian employers do not expect a photo, your date of birth or your marital status.
  • Employment history: start with your most recent role. Use action verbs and add numbers when you can.
  • Licences and certificates: list the ones the job asks for, such as a driver licence, White Card, RSA, First Aid or a Working with Children Check.
  • Referees: list at least two people who can talk about your work, such as a former manager or supervisor. Do not use family members, and always ask before you list someone.
  • Spelling: use Australian spelling, such as organise, licence and colour.

Which template should I pick?

The classic resume suits nearly every job. The modern template is handy when you want your target role and strongest skills at the top, for example after a change of career. The no-experience template is for your first casual job, a traineeship or an apprenticeship: school, sport and volunteering all count.

Before you send your resume

Ask someone to proofread it for spelling and grammar. Save it as a PDF (File, Save As, PDF) unless the job ad asks for Word, and give it a clear name such as "Firstname-Lastname-Resume.pdf". Tailor your summary and skills to each job ad, and address the selection criteria if there are any.

Tips for editing the template in Word

To add a bullet point, press Enter at the end of an existing one. To move a whole section, select its heading and the lines below it, then cut and paste. If your text spills onto a second page, shorten older jobs to one or two bullet points before you make the font smaller: 10 points is about as small as you can go and stay easy to read. The page is set to A4, so it prints correctly on standard paper, and the margins are about 1.8 cm.

Frequently asked questions

Are these Word resume templates really free?

Yes. No account, no email and no payment: download the .docx file and use it for your applications.

Is a resume the same as a CV in Australia?

Mostly, yes. Australians use both words for the document you send with a job application. Resume is more common, and CV is often used for longer academic or medical profiles.

How many referees should I put on my resume?

At least two, usually people who have managed or supervised you. Ask them first, and do not list family members.
