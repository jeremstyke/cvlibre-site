What to include in a resignation letter

  • The date.
  • A clear sentence saying that you are resigning from your position.
  • Your notice period and your last day of work.
  • A short thank you and an offer to help with the handover.
  • Your signature.

You do not have to give a reason for leaving. Keep the letter short, positive and factual: you may need a reference from this employer one day.

How much notice do I need to give?

Your notice period is usually set by your employment contract, your award or your enterprise agreement. Check them first: the Fair Work Ombudsman explains the rules for your situation.

Frequently asked questions

Will I be paid for my unused annual leave?

Yes, unused annual leave is paid out in your final pay when you leave.

Can I get a statement of service?

You can ask your employer for one. It confirms your role and dates of employment, which is useful for your next job.
