How to write a job application email

  • A clear subject line: "Application for [job title] - [your name]".
  • One sentence saying which job you are applying for and where you saw it.
  • Two or three strengths that match the job.
  • A mention of your attached resume and cover letter.

Name your files clearly, for example CV_First_Last.pdf, and send them as PDFs so the layout does not change.
