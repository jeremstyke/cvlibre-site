How to fill out this resume template

Replace every text in square brackets with your own details, then delete the brackets. The headings and bullets use real Word styles, so to add a job you can copy an existing block and paste it below: the formatting comes with it.

  • Header: your name, city and state, phone, email and a LinkedIn or portfolio link. CareerOneStop notes that you don't need your full street address.
  • No photo, no age: US resumes leave out a photo, your date of birth and your marital status. Employers expect your skills and results, not personal details.
  • Summary: three or four sentences, or a few bullets, about your experience and what you bring to the role. With three years of experience or less, it's optional.
  • Experience: most recent job first. Start each bullet with an action verb and add a number when you can: sales, customers, time saved, team size.
  • One page: that's the norm for most US job seekers. Go to two pages only with a long, relevant career.
  • References: keep them off the resume. Prepare a separate list and share it when the employer asks.
  • Key words: many employers use applicant tracking systems. Reuse the exact words from the job posting when they are true for you.

Which template should I pick?

The classic resume works for nearly every job, from retail and warehouse roles to office positions. The modern template suits people who want a target job title and their core skills right at the top. The entry-level template is for a first job, an internship or a summer job: class projects, clubs and volunteer work all count.

Before you send your resume

Proofread it twice, then save it as a PDF (File, Save As, PDF) unless the posting asks for a Word file, so the layout looks the same on the employer's screen. Name the file clearly, for example "Firstname-Lastname-Resume.pdf". Adjust your summary and the order of your skills for each posting.

Tips for editing the template in Word

To add a bullet point, press Enter at the end of an existing one. To move a whole section, select its heading and the lines below it, then cut and paste. If your text spills onto a second page, shorten older jobs to one or two bullet points before you make the font smaller: 10 points is about as small as you can go and stay easy to read. The page is set to US Letter, so it prints correctly on standard paper, and the margins are about 0.7 inch.

Frequently asked questions

Are these Word resume templates really free?

Yes. No account, no email and no payment: download the .docx file and use it for your job applications.

Should a US resume include a photo?

No. Most US employers don't expect a photo, and leaving it off keeps attention on your skills. Leave out your age, date of birth and marital status too.

Can I use these templates in Google Docs?

Yes. Upload the .docx file to Google Drive and open it with Google Docs. Check the layout, then download it as a PDF.
