How to fill out this resume template

Replace every text in square brackets with your own details, then delete the brackets. The headings and bullets use real Word styles: to add a job, copy an existing block and paste it underneath, and the formatting follows.

  • Two pages at most: Job Bank, the Government of Canada's job site, recommends limiting your resume to two pages. These templates fit on one page with room to grow.
  • No photo: Job Bank says not to include a picture of yourself. Also leave out your age, height, weight, marital status and religion, and never write your Social Insurance Number.
  • Languages: bilingualism is an asset for many jobs in Canada. Give your level in English and in French, and add any other language you speak.
  • Experience: most recent job first. Use action verbs and add numbers. If you worked abroad, name the city and country, and explain any job title an employer here might not know.
  • Education earned abroad: if you have a credential assessment, mention the Canadian equivalent.
  • References: Job Bank advises keeping them off the resume, on a separate sheet you hand over when an employer asks.
  • Spelling: use Canadian spelling, such as colour, centre and licence (the noun), with organize and recognize.

Which template should I pick?

The classic resume fits most jobs, in any province. The modern template puts your target job and your languages up front, which helps for customer service and public-facing roles. The template with no experience is for a co-op placement, a summer job or your first job after high school or college.

The elegant and two-column designs stand out when you email your resume or hand it in. For online application systems, the classic template is the safest choice, because some of them read columns in the wrong order.

Before you send your resume

Proofread it several times. Save it as a PDF (File, Save As, PDF) unless the employer asks for Word, and give the file a clear name such as "Firstname-Lastname-Resume.pdf". Adjust your profile and skills to each job posting: the same key words help with applicant tracking systems.

Tips for editing the template in Word

To add a bullet point, press Enter at the end of an existing one. To move a whole section, select its heading and the lines below it, then cut and paste. If your text spills onto a second page, shorten older jobs to one or two bullet points before you make the font smaller: 10 points is about as small as you can go and stay easy to read. The page is set to Letter size, the standard paper format in Canada, so it prints correctly on standard paper, and the margins are about 0.7 inch (1.8 cm).

Frequently asked questions

Are these Word resume templates really free?

Yes. No account, no email and no payment: download the .docx file and use it for your applications.

Should a Canadian resume include references?

Not on the resume itself. Job Bank suggests listing them on a separate sheet and giving it to the employer when asked.

Should I put a photo on my resume in Canada?

No. A photo is not the norm in Canada, and Job Bank advises against it. Leave out your age and marital status too.
